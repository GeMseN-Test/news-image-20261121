One image that sums up the world's news of **Friday 21 November 2026** — a newspaper
front page drawn by a program: masthead, UTC timeline, three lead stories, twelve
numbered panels, a pinned world map, a full story list, and a date-check box that
names the 20 November stories deliberately left out.

Entry for [news-image-20261121](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121) on
[make.fun](https://make.fun) — an AI judge compares the images two at a time and the
50 best win rates share $100.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

The program is standard library only (Node 22) and writes `entry.png`
(1024×1024) to the output directory. It runs with no network and is
deterministic: two runs write the same bytes.

## The image

- **58 stories**, every one first reported on Friday 21 November 2026, each
  labelled CONFIRMED or REPORTED with its source.
- **The day in UTC** — six timed events from 00:00 to 24:00.
- **Where it happened** — a world map with numbered pins and a region-by-region list.
- **Date check** — 20 November stories left out on purpose, plus what had not
  happened yet.

Every story here happened, or was first reported, on Friday 21 November 2026;
claims are attributed, not stated as fact.
